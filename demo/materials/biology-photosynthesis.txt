Course: BIO101 - Introductory Biology
Lecture: Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. In plants it occurs in chloroplasts. Chlorophyll absorbs light.
Light-dependent reactions occur in thylakoid membranes. They produce ATP and NADPH and release oxygen from water.
The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma. It uses ATP and NADPH to support carbon fixation from carbon dioxide.
Plants also carry out cellular respiration. Photosynthesis and respiration are distinct processes.
Common misconception: the oxygen released during photosynthesis comes from carbon dioxide. It comes from water.
